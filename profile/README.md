# Sievehouse

**Building toward more reliable, measurable, and verifiable GPU infrastructure.**

Sievehouse explores software opportunities across GPU and AI infrastructure, with a focus on reliability, independent verification, and evidence-based performance assurance.

## Our Focus

Our current research investigates how GPU cloud customers and providers can verify delivered compute quality against contractual requirements.

- **GPU Delivery Assurance** — Exploring acceptance testing and measurement of delivered GPU capacity.
- **SLA Evidence** — Investigating reliable, contract-aware records of infrastructure performance.
- **Infrastructure Security** — Exploring tenant-isolation assurance for GPU clouds.
- **Compute Risk** — Studying infrastructure performance evidence relevant to lenders and insurers.

## Our Approach

We prioritize independent research, reproducible measurements, open-source tooling, and evidence-driven validation. We test assumptions before committing to building a product.

Our current direction is a research-backed opportunity, not a claim of an established commercial offering.
